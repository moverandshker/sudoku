New puzzle

Gentle needs only singles. Steady adds locked candidates and pairs. Tough needs triples, X-wings and XY-wings. Fiendish needs XYZ-wings, swordfish or colouring. Every puzzle is graded by the techniques required to solve it and only released if its score lands inside a narrow window — so your times are comparable within a band.

About

This app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no remote config, no fonts or scripts loaded from anywhere. Puzzles are generated on your device; your history is stored in this browser's local storage and nowhere else. Delete the app or clear site data and it is gone. Export your log any time from Stats.
